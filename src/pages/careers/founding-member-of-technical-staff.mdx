## About Parallax

[Parallax](https://parallx.ai/) is a new UK-based non-profit research organisation developing open methods and infrastructure for white-box auditing of frontier AI systems. Our mission is to make evidence about models’ internal beliefs, goals, and plans a core component of AI evaluations, allowing evaluators to identify, explain, and predict safety-relevant failures more reliably.

We conduct open research to benefit society. We publish our findings, release our methods and infrastructure, and aim to make scientific contributions that remain useful as agentic capabilities of frontier models keep increasing. You can read more about our mission, strategy, and team on our [About page](https://parallx.ai/about.html).

## About the role

We are recruiting two Members of Technical Staff to join Parallax as founding members of our technical team. As an early member of a fast-moving, highly collaborative team, you will take substantial ownership of projects developing frontier white-box AI auditing technology, from ideation to implementation, evaluation and release. You will also play a fundamental role in defining Parallax's character and impact as it scales into a leading European actor in this space.

Our existing collaborations include the [National Deep Inference Fabric (NDIF)](https://ndif.us/) at Northeastern University and [Cohere](https://cohere.com/)'s AI Safety team. We also plan to engage with organisations across the evaluation and interpretability ecosystem, including the [UK AI Security Institute](https://www.aisi.gov.uk/), [Meridian Labs](https://meridianlabs.ai/), [Goodfire](https://www.goodfire.com/), and [METR](https://metr.org/).

## About our research

You will lead research efforts to elicit and test high-level latent variables to explain model behaviour, including beliefs about the current and future action-conditioned environment states, plans, goals, and motivational structures. Examples of our current priorities include:

* Improving and validating meta-models such as [activation oracles](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.15674) to decode model representations into concise, testable descriptions.
* Developing counterfactual training methods to improve meta-models’ predictivity, robustness and causal sensitivity.
* Creating benchmarks to validate whether elicited beliefs reliably predict model behaviours, distinguishing between competing explanations for different failure categories.
* Developing scalable methods for locating pivotal steps for efficient belief elicitation in long-horizon agent trajectories.
* Building open research infrastructure to make proposed methods reproducible and usable by other evaluators.

As a founding technical team member, you will help refine these questions and shape Parallax’s broader research agenda.

## Responsibilities

Your main responsibility will be to proactively identify and execute impactful research alongside the Parallax team. This includes:

* Taking ownership of substantial research lines, from scoping and prototyping through experimental validation and release.
* Collaborating with founders to formulate clear scientific questions and design rigorous experimental protocols to validate them.
* Designing and implementing efficient and reliable training and inference pipelines, supporting fast iteration while preserving legibility and reusability.
* Running large-scale experiments using inference APIs and multi-node GPU infrastructure.
* Contributing to write-ups, benchmarks and open-source software to share the results of your analyses, and presenting your research at conferences and research convenings.
* Helping supervise research interns, partnering with external collaborators and contributing to the development of future members of the technical staff.
* Participating in decisions about Parallax’s research direction, technical standards, and organisational development.
