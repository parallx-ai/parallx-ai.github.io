# Hero watercolor

`watercolor.webp` is the local background plate for `Hero.astro`. Created on 2026-09-05 using the built-in image generation tool, with the user's attached Parallax banner as a style reference. The generated PNG was converted to WebP at quality 85. The spheres, glass shells, circuits, and interaction are separate HTML/CSS/SVG in `HeroSphere.astro`.

The sphere shells use SVG pigment grain and feathered masks. `HeroOrbits.astro` draws gold orbital paths and connections that follow the floating spheres, with a soft mask behind the hero text. These effects are rendered in code and do not change the background image.

`HeroRays.astro` and `src/scripts/hero-spheres.ts` coordinate the interactive reveal: a filled golden beam narrows from a wide base at the hero's top edge to a point at the selected sphere, reveals its circuitry at impact, and forks into two widening beams toward the lower edge. Animated SVG masks reveal the triangular beams in sequence, then the beams fade together. Hover and keyboard focus only enlarge the sphere. Click, tap, Enter, or Space toggles its reveal; selecting another sphere or pressing Escape dismisses it. Reduced motion reveals the circuitry immediately without a ray.

Mouse, touch, and pen drags move the sphere and its orbital ring with a heavy initial response and a logarithmic travel curve: equal pointer movements produce progressively smaller sphere movements farther from the origin, without a fixed boundary. Resistance scales with sphere size and keeps even long pulls close to the origin. A six-pixel movement threshold distinguishes dragging from clicking. Releasing returns it smoothly over two seconds; re-grabbing continues from its current position and preserves resistance. Dragging interrupts an active ray without triggering a new reveal. The return loop runs only while needed and stops when the hero is hidden; reduced motion returns immediately. Ray geometry is measured on activation and resize, with tracked drag offsets keeping a ray aligned during a return without per-frame layout reads. Touch gestures beginning on a sphere drag it; the rest of the hero scrolls normally.
